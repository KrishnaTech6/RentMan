# The landlord creates every bill; the app only prompts

The app never creates a bill on its own. It lists unbilled months as to-dos and sends a monthly notification, and the landlord creates each bill, entering a meter reading where electricity is billed. A reader might expect bills to generate automatically on the 1st, but mobile apps can't run reliably in the background, and a bill needs a human-entered reading for electricity. Skipped months stay on the to-do list, and pending counts only bills that exist.
