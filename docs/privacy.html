Privacy policy

Rent Manager · last updated 27 November 2026

What the app keeps

The renters, addresses, bills, payments, meter readings, meter photos and settings you enter. All of it is stored on your phone.

Where it goes

  • Nowhere, unless you choose. The app has no server and no account with us. We never see your data.
  • Google Drive backup (optional). If you connect Google Drive, the app saves a backup of your data and meter photos into your own Google Drive. It asks only for access to the files it creates itself, so it cannot see or change anything else in your Drive. You can disconnect at any time in Settings; the backup files stay in your Drive until you delete them.
  • Backup files you share. "Back up now" creates a file and opens your phone's share menu. Where it goes is your choice.
  • WhatsApp. When you send a bill or a payment note, the app opens WhatsApp with the message typed in. You pick the chat and press send.

Camera and photos

Meter photos are taken or chosen by you. The reading is read from the photo on your phone; the photo is not sent anywhere unless you back up to Google Drive.

What we do not do

No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no selling or sharing of data. The app does not collect anything about you or your renters for us.

Google user data

Data from your Google account is used only to save and restore your own backup in your own Google Drive, as described above, and is not used for anything else. The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting your data

Delete the app to remove everything on the phone. Delete the "Rent Manager" folder in your Google Drive to remove the backup.
